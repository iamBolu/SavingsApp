◆  Logo studies

Five marks. Pick one.

Each concept is shown on light and dark, at wordmark scale. All are hand-drawn SVG — whichever you choose gets refined into a full icon set (favicon, app icon, social).

Concept 01

The Question Coin

✓ Selected

A coin with a question mark struck where the denomination should be. The whole product in one image: money that asks first. Reads clearly at 16px favicon size.

Concept 02

The Pause

Two upright bars — a pause button — with an ember diamond between them. The brand gesture is the moment of hesitation before buying. Quietest of the five; very “serious fintech.”

Concept 03

The Speech Cheque

A speech bubble with a checkmark inside — the conversation and the approval in one shape. Friendliest of the set; suits the chat-first interface best.

Concept 04

ask. — the editorial wordmark

No symbol at all — just the word “ask” in Fraunces italic with an ember full stop. The period is the decision. Extremely distinctive in a category full of shields and piggy banks; works beautifully in the editorial layout.

Concept 05

The Ledger Slash

A rounded square tile holding “A/S” split by a diagonal — Ask over Spend, the question literally standing above the purchase. The tile shape drops straight into an app icon with zero rework.

✓ Concept 01 — The Question Coin — is the chosen mark. It now lives in the site header and favicon. The other four studies stay here for the record.
