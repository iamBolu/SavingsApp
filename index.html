01 · The flip

Budgeting apps are rearview mirrors.

They show you where the money went, after it's already gone. Ask Before You Spend looks forward instead: it checks the road ahead of every purchase, so regret never makes it to your statement.

  1. 01

    You ask, in plain words

    "Can I afford new AirPods this weekend?" No categories, no spreadsheets. Just the question you were already asking yourself.

  2. 02

    It does the honest math

    Balance minus the bills due before payday, minus what's reserved for your goals, weighed against how you've actually been spending.

  3. 03

    You get a straight answer

    Yes, not yet, or wait until Friday. The reasoning is shown, so the answer is one you can trust and argue with.
